Opportunities by pool:
  SELECT pool_name, COUNT(*), AVG(estimated_profit_usd)

Recent transactions:
  SELECT * FROM transactions ORDER BY timestamp DESC LIMIT 20;

Competition per block:
  SELECT block_number, COUNT(*) as tx_count
  FROM transactions GROUP BY block_number
